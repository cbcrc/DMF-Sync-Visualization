Sync maps onto a Flow Synchronization Group: `FlowSynchronizationGroup::waitForDataAt()` waits until every reader in the group has a head at or past the grain for one timestamp. A "live" input is a reader outside that group: it is not waited on and reads at `runtime.headIndex`, so the model (read at its own write head, never holds the function back) is right. Also confirmed: a reader may ask for any index up to the head (`getGrainImpl`: `in_index <= headIndex`), the oldest readable index is `head - grainCount + 2`, and the head grain can be read once its slices are committed (`getGrain` with `minValidSlices`), so reading one grain behind (`READ_BEHIND_WRITE_GRAINS`) is conservative for whole-grain writes.
